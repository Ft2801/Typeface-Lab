# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone who participates in the Typeface Lab community, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Communicating respectfully and constructively.
- Giving and accepting technical feedback in good faith.
- Focusing discussion on the project and its users.
- Respecting different viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this policy. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Conduct concerns should be reported privately to the repository owner through the contact options available on the [Ft2801 GitHub profile](https://github.com/Ft2801). Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies to all project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other public or private interactions made on behalf of the project.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
